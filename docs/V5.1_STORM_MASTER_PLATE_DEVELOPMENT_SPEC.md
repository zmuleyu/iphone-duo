## Executive Summary

This branch does not redesign the iPhone Duo model and does not modify the accepted production fold geometry. It reuses the Tokyo Tower production toolchain as the fixed physical stage and adds one isolated content mode:

**STORM II choreography plate → AI character replacement → final composite**

The immediate objective is to turn the existing 5.8-second Greybox into a production-grade white-model master plate that can be used directly as a motion/composition reference for AI video tools such as Seedance.

The branch must export five synchronized deliverables from one deterministic choreography timeline:

1. Master Plate — Duo + white-model characters + system elements.
2. Clean Plate — Duo + stage/environment, without characters.
3. Character Mask — clean character silhouettes / optional ID-color mask.
4. Motion Reference — characters and major props isolated for AI motion/reference workflows.
5. Hero Frame — full-open freeze-frame still suitable as an X image and as an image-to-video reference.

Core principle: **Duo geometry remains deterministic; AI only replaces or enhances the performance layer.**

## 3. Narrative rule

Opening the Duo must not simply reveal more canvas. It must cause the world to reorganize itself. The transferable STORM II mechanics are convergence, formation locking, one lead figure with minimal motion, a collective pulse, a short hold before climax, and compression back to a compact state.

The Duo’s two physical display planes become the stage architecture.

## 4. Master 5.8s timeline

- 0.00–0.75 RUMOR: closed; Founder offset right; partial card + one orbit only.
- 0.75–1.65 PULL: Founder moves toward hinge; Compute enters; UI/Orbit actively reposition.
- 1.65–3.00 FORMATION: Seated/two-plane stage forms; anchors, Chorus, robots and system props lock.
- 3.00–3.45 HOLD: spatial motion settles; Founder completes 3/4 → front.
- 3.45–4.55 PULSE: Founder → Front Chorus → Rear Chorus → Robots/System cascade.
- 4.55–5.05 FREEZE: ~500 ms; hero-frame interval.
- 5.05–5.80 COMPRESSION: slight close; world compresses to Founder + one orbit + AI/system glyph.

Recommended pulse timing:
Founder 3.56s; Front Chorus 3.66s; Rear Chorus 3.74s; Robots/System 3.82s.

All character and prop motion should derive from F and the deterministic STORM timeline.

## 7. Formation

Full-open anchor triangle:

Founder at primary visual anchor; Compute and Conversation flank it with distinct body language.

Chorus uses two rows of three. Avoid perfectly uniform spacing, but unify them during Pulse.

Upper plane = people/robots/orbital world.
Lower plane = control/vehicle/fabrication/system world.

## 8. Render passes

All passes share identical duration, frame rate, camera, fold motion, choreography timing and crop.

**Master:** Duo + white/grey characters + system props.

**Clean:** Duo + stage/environment; no human performers and no obstructive foreground props.

**Mask:** black background + flat white character silhouettes; later optional ID-color mask.

**Motion:** neutral grey background + characters/robots only; prioritize readable pose and motion.

**Hero Frame:** capture near 4.82s during FREEZE.

## 10. AI/Seedance handoff

The Duo tool owns geometry, camera, fold angle, choreography blocking, timing, masks and deterministic reference outputs.

The AI-video tool owns final character appearance, secondary performance, clothing motion, subtle expression and limited cinematic enhancement.

Never ask the AI-video tool to reinvent the iPhone Duo device.

First replacement test:
Founder placeholder → Elon-style Founder, using Motion Reference + Founder mask/crop + character reference image + white-shirt STORM target.

Only after that succeeds move to a three-character test. Keep Chorus anonymous until the mechanism is stable.
