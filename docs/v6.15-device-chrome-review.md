## Verified differences and bounded fixes

1. V6.14 drew the Tokyo lock-screen chrome with a local Canvas approximation. Its clock hierarchy, Wi-Fi symbol, utility-control scale and home indicator differed from the prepared Apple overlays already used by the default Wallpaper UI. V6.15 reuses those prepared `clock-inner.avif` and `clock-outer.avif` overlays for Tokyo custom worlds.
2. V6.14 baked both right-rail key clusters at `+0.08x`; the controls remained visibly detached from the rail in the supplied Closed/Open references. V6.15 seats both clusters at `+0.04x` while preserving the two-key arrangement.
3. The top-edge tabs, hinge, panels, Screen UV, fixed camera, fold motion and frame clock remain unchanged. The prepared Apple USDZ is the geometry authority; this stage does not reinterpret stock top-edge or hinge geometry.

Tokyo Reality/RedBlack assets, reveal logic, montage, birds, tower effects, text and sound are unchanged.
